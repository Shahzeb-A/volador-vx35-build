Build record

About this build

This is a custom assembled 3.5 inch FPV quadcopter built on the FlyFishRC Volador VX3.5 airframe. It was put together by the operator from off the shelf parts rather than bought as a finished drone, which is why it carries an operator applied serial number for registration.

The platform pairs 1804 motors on 3.5 inch tri blade propellers with a SpeedyBee style mini stack running Betaflight, fed by a 4S lithium polymer pack through an XT60 lead. Video is handled by an analog 5.8 GHz transmitter, with a BetaFPV × Insta360 action camera carried on top for recorded footage. It is flown within visual line of sight for recreation and aerial filming.

All specifications and photographs on this page describe the actual aircraft and are kept here as a build and registration record.
